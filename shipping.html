Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Zander88LLC ships orders placed at www.zander88llc.net. We currently focus on delivery within the United States. Shipping options and any promotional offers shown at checkout apply to that order.

Order processing

Orders are typically prepared after payment is confirmed. Processing usually takes 1–3 business days, not counting weekends or public holidays. During peak periods it may take longer. You will receive order updates by email when tracking is available.

If an item is out of stock after checkout, we will contact you and either wait for restock, ship the available items, or refund the unavailable portion.

Shipping cost

Complimentary U.S. delivery may apply on qualifying orders of $75 or more, as shown on the site and at checkout. Orders under that amount are charged the shipping rate displayed before you pay.

Promotional shipping offers can change and cannot be added after an order is placed unless we say otherwise.

Delivery estimates

Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees. Transit time depends on the carrier, destination, weather, and regional delays. Once the order is with the carrier, tracking is the best guide to its location.

Risk of loss passes to you when the carrier delivers the package to the address provided at checkout.

Address accuracy

Please confirm your shipping name, street address, city, state, and ZIP code before paying. We are not responsible for delays or lost packages caused by an incomplete or incorrect address.

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable, we will contact you. Reshipping may require an additional charge.

Delays

Carriers, weather, natural events, or other circumstances outside our reasonable control can delay delivery. If your order is unusually late, email us with your order details so we can look into it.

Lost or damaged packages

If a shipment arrives damaged, incomplete, or does not arrive, contact us promptly at curtis@zander88llc.net with your order number and photos if the item is damaged. We will review the claim and, where appropriate, arrange a replacement or refund.

For return steps after delivery, see our Returns & Refunds policy.

Contact

Shipping questions can be sent to curtis@zander88llc.net with your order number.
