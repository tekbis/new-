Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Zander88LLC (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates www.zander88llc.net. This policy describes the personal information we handle when you use the store. By shopping with us, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or place an order.

We collect only what we need to run the shop, complete orders, prevent fraud, and keep the experience reliable.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email, phone number, and shipping or billing address at checkout
  • Order details, cart contents, and messages you send us
  • Account or newsletter details if you choose to share them

Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, and general location data
  • Pages viewed, products selected, and how you move through the store
  • IP address and basic security logs

We do not ask children under 13 to provide personal information.

How we use information

  • Process orders, payments, shipping, and customer support
  • Confirm purchases and send order updates
  • Keep the store secure and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Remember your cart and improve the website
  • Send store updates only when you have asked for them

Payments

Card and PayPal payments are processed by Stripe and PayPal. We do not store full card numbers on our servers. Those providers handle payment details under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and similar tools

We use cookies and local storage to keep your cart, remember preferences, and understand store traffic. You can block cookies in your browser, but checkout and cart features may not work correctly without them.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Payment processors, including Stripe and PayPal
  • Shipping and fulfillment partners needed to deliver your order
  • Service providers who help us host, secure, or operate the store
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the store and our customers

How long we keep information

Order and customer records are kept as long as needed to complete the purchase, handle returns, meet tax or legal duties, and resolve disputes. You may ask us to update or delete information that we are not required to keep.

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and trusted payment partners to protect your information. No website is completely risk-free, so please keep your devices and passwords secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, or ask us not to use it for marketing. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. Email us and we will respond as required by law.

We may update this policy when the store or the law changes. The date at the top of this page will change, and the new version applies when it is posted.
